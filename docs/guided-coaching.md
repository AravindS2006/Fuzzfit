# Guided workouts and live coaching

Choose Camera coaching for private practice, or open an enrolled class and join live video. A coach starts the class, chooses its exercise, and can pause the class, focus an individual, send private corrections, respond to help requests, and mute trainee microphones. Trainees control their own camera and microphone. Coaches cannot turn someone’s microphone on remotely.

## Trainee flow

1. Select the exercise and read its compact movement cue. Open **Workout settings** for full camera and movement instructions; illustrations are not used.
2. Choose sets, reps (seconds for plank holds), and rest. An assigned workout plan supplies the starting targets. Exercise preferences are saved in this browser. Adjust the working side, comfortable joint endpoints, and minimum rep duration with your coach when needed.
3. Follow the selected exercise's camera-view instruction in good lighting. Biceps curls support front-facing phones and slight side views. Squats, push-ups, lunges, rows, bridges, and crunches use a side view. Lateral raises and jumping jacks use a front view. Keep the listed joints and space around your body visible. In a live class, the published camera also supplies local analysis.
4. Enable the camera and follow positioning feedback. **Accurate** uses the Full pose model by default; **High precision** selects Heavy for faster devices, and **Performance** selects Lite. Optional voice and rep sounds can be enabled. Rehearsal does not add workout reps or form-score samples.
5. Press **Start set** while near the device, then step into the starting position. The five-second countdown tolerates small movement and pauses for brief uncertainty; sustained lost positioning resets it. Begin moving after **Go**. The app shows cumulative reps, current-set progress, and a posture estimate. During an active set, open **Workout settings** for the completed rep's quality, working side, estimated joint-angle range, duration, and tracking feedback. Planks accumulate observed aligned time.
6. Completing the target starts a rest timer. Start the next set when ready, skip rest, or finish a set early. Results show the amount completed and average observed posture score. Set details stay in the current page; joined-class cumulative rep/form summaries are saved in Insights. Private practice does not write to the server.

## Tracking rules

`profile-v3` uses pinned MediaPipe Tasks Vision in a worker, one pose per trainee camera, and native VIDEO tracking. Full is the default; GPU initialization falls back to CPU when unavailable. Fresh video frames are scheduled with `requestVideoFrameCallback` when supported, with one inference in flight and a 33 ms polling fallback on older browsers. Actual analyzed FPS depends on the device. Image coordinates are corrected for the video's aspect ratio. The selected exercise's required joints must have at least 0.65 confidence and remain in frame.

A rep requires an observed starting position, movement through the configured opposite endpoint, and an observed return. Starting positions follow the exercise's direction: curls begin with the arm extended, while presses begin with the elbow bent. Brief endpoint reversals do not require a prolonged hold. Adaptive smoothing, hysteresis, observed range, duration, technique, and cooldown checks prevent incomplete or duplicate counts. Minimum durations come from each exercise profile and can be adjusted with the coach.

Automatic limb selection can choose the moving arm at the start of a front-facing curl, then locks that limb through the cycle. Trustworthy world coordinates help track motion toward the camera; segment plausibility and stability are checked, and geometry is kept consistent during a cycle. Brief occlusion freezes the cycle without counting missing time. Sustained loss, a changed camera/person view, or pauses require reacquiring the start. A return completed entirely out of view is not counted. Implausible joint jumps are rejected. Hold time is accumulated only between consecutive eligible observations, with exact milliseconds retained across sets.

The catalog contains twelve selected-exercise profiles: bodyweight squat, push-up, biceps curl, plank, stationary lunge, shoulder press, lateral raise, jumping jack, glute bridge, controlled crunch, bent-over row, and side plank. Jumping jacks require both arm movement and feet opening/closing. Front-view and floor-position checks distinguish several common incorrect setups. Each profile has its own required joints, direction, range, timing, and coaching cues.

Minor form issues lower completed rep quality and produce corrections; clearly invalid technique or incomplete movement does not count. Rep quality combines observed form, range, confidence, and control. It is an estimate, not a clinical safety assessment or a claim that every joint is correctly positioned. The app analyzes the exercise selected by the trainee or coach; it does not classify every possible workout automatically. Depth, camera angle, clothing, occlusion, and device performance affect estimates. Variations require coach review and appropriate configuration. See [the twelve-layer implementation](pose-engine-v3.md) and [research notes](pose-research-notes.md).

## Video lifecycle

Prejoin preview starts only after an explicit click, supports camera and microphone selection, and releases its tracks on exit or join. The microphone starts muted unless the participant chooses otherwise. Device selection, camera switching, fullscreen, adjustable coach video, optional classmates, participant focus, active-speaker indicators, help requests, private cues, and class chat support group instruction. **Small** keeps the coach in a compact square over the trainee's camera; minimizing retains audio and a restore control. Compact expandable cues and tracking details inside settings preserve camera space on phones.

Room events reuse the same local MediaStream while its underlying camera track is unchanged. Remote joins, mute changes, and speaker events therefore do not restart pose analysis. Only the conferencing controls own published tracks; stopping the local analyzer leaves published video running. Cloud regional HTTPS and WebSocket hosts are permitted by the browser connection policy, consistent with [LiveKit’s firewall guidance](https://docs.livekit.io/deploy/admin/firewall/). Reconnection and blocked audio are explained in the UI.

Pause/resume preserves the exercise revision and cumulative metrics. Exercise changes create a new revision. The API validates enrollment, sharing consent, class state, revision, monotonic counters, and plausible increments before saving metrics. Coach-only muting validates session ownership and enrolled identities on the server.

## Verification

Unit regressions cover ten continuous curls, front-facing and slight-angle phone views, left/right selection, depth motion, different frame rates, partial and fast reps, limb-confidence changes, configured endpoints, invalid positions, tracking loss, and observed hold time. Exercise-specific tests cover the new profiles and several incorrect setups. Browser tests cover mobile dismissal, persistent setup preferences, permission denial, the real MediaPipe model/worker, hands-free countdowns, and deterministic guided sets with cumulative counts. API tests cover authorization, consent, private messages, pause/resume, stale metrics, and export totals. Accessibility checks include mobile navigation and both account roles.

Deterministic landmark tests verify counting logic; a blank simulated camera verifies model loading, not real-exercise accuracy. Physical camera trials across different bodies, phones, lighting, and exercise variations remain necessary to measure detection accuracy.
